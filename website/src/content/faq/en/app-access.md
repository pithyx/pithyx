---
question: 'What can an app see of my files and data?'
category: apps
tags: [app permissions, privacy, sandbox, files, internet access, app security]
order: 50
status: ready
---

Only what its permissions allow, and never more than your own account may do. Without file permissions an app sees no files; with them it reads files you open with it or pick in a dialog, or browses the shared folders you may use. Without the permission **Internet** it cannot leave the box. An administrator sees the permissions before installing and can switch them off.

Details: [What an app may access](/docs/user/apps/#what-an-app-may-access).
