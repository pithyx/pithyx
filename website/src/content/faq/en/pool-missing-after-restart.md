---
question: 'My pool is missing after a restart. What now?'
category: storage
tags: [pool missing, pool gone, restart, reboot, import, disks missing, degraded]
order: 10
status: ready
---

A pool you created or imported for use comes back at every boot, as long as its disks are there. If it is missing or shows missing disks, check cables and power of the disks and restart; shared folders and apps on it stay unavailable until it is back. A pool you only imported read-only as a preview is not kept, and after a reinstall the box does not know your pools: import them with **Import a pool…** in Storage.

Details: [Importing and exporting](/docs/admin/storage/#importing-and-exporting).
