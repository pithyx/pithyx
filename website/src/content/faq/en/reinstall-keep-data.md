question: 'Does reinstalling Pithyx delete my data?'
category: install
tags: [reinstall, data loss, pools, backup, restore, system disk]
order: 70
status: ready
---

Not if you choose only the system disks: the installer erases the disks you select and nothing else. Afterwards import your pools in Storage with **Import a pool…** (a forced import is normal after a reinstall) and restore a configuration backup in Settings > **Backup and restore**. The installer does neither for you.

Details: [Recovery and reinstall](/docs/install/recovery/#reinstalling).
