---
question: 'How do I give someone the details of a problem on my box?'
category: general
tags: [support bundle, help, bug report, diagnostics, logs, memory dump, crash report]
order: 70
status: ready
---

Make a support bundle: an administrator chooses **Support bundle** in the Log Center's toolbar, picks the parts and downloads the zip file. It never holds passwords, keys or tokens, but it does hold user names, folder names, addresses and host names, so look at it before you share it. The box keeps it for an hour and never sends it anywhere by itself.

Details: [Support bundles](/docs/admin/logs-and-monitoring/#support-bundles).
