There is no separate mobile app. The web desktop adapts to the screen: on a phone each app fills the screen, on a tablet windows are maximized. For files, any app that speaks SMB 3, SFTP, WebDAV or FTP with TLS can connect.

Details: [Phones and tablets](/docs/user/desktop/#phones-and-tablets).
