---
question: 'Where did my deleted file go?'
category: files
tags: [recycle bin, trash, deleted, undelete, restore file]
order: 20
status: ready
---

Into the recycle bin of its shared folder, if you deleted it in Files, over SMB or over WebDAV. Open **Recycle bin** in the sidebar of Files and choose **Restore**. Items stay there for 30 days by default. **Delete permanently** (Shift+Del) skips the bin, and deleting over SFTP or FTP may skip it too.

Details: [The recycle bin](/docs/user/files/#the-recycle-bin).
