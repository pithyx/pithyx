No. Pithyx has no telemetry, no phone-home and no cloud account, and logs stay on the box. The only connections it makes on its own fetch the enabled app catalogs and their images, reach public time servers unless you set your own, and those of apps you allow the permission **Internet**.

Details: [Privacy](/docs/project/privacy/).
