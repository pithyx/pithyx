A 64-bit x86 computer (x86-64-v2 or newer) with UEFI, at least 2 GB of memory (4 GB or more recommended) and an internal system disk of at least 32 GB. The installer checks all of this before it starts. The reference machine is an Intel N100 or N305 with 8 GB of memory.

Details: [Hardware requirements](/docs/getting-started/requirements/).
