question: 'Do users have a private folder of their own?'
category: files

Yes. Every user has a home folder that only they and the administrators can open. It is **home** in Files and over SMB, SFTP, WebDAV and FTP; administrators also see every user's home below **homes**. It has its own recycle bin. Administrators choose its pool and quota in Settings > **Home folders**.

Details: [Home folders](/docs/admin/home-folders/).
