---
question: 'Why is my disk "Not available" or asks for its serial number?'

A disk is **Not available** when it holds the system, is already in a pool, is read-only or in use, shares its serial number with another disk, or holds data but has no serial number; the Storage app shows the reason. A disk with partitions or data **Needs confirmation**: type its serial number to use it, and everything on it is erased.

Details: [Disk verdicts](/docs/admin/storage/#disk-verdicts).
