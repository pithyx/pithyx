---
question: 'Can I add disks to a pool later?'
category: storage
tags: [add disk, expand pool, grow pool, raid-z expansion, vdev, single to mirror]
order: 40
status: ready
---

Yes, in two ways: **Add disks…** adds a new group of disks to the pool, and **Expand with a disk…** widens a RAID-Z group by one disk. Both are irreversible. Settings has no button to turn a single disk into a mirror later.

Details: [Growing a pool](/docs/admin/storage/#growing-a-pool).
