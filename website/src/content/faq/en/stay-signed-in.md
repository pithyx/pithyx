---
question: 'How long do I stay signed in?'
category: users
tags: [session, signed out, logout, cookie, session expired, other devices]
order: 70
status: ready
---

Up to 7 days without using the desktop and at most 30 days in total. **Sign out** ends the session of this browser only; there is no list of sessions and no way to sign out other devices yourself. A new password set by an administrator ends all your sessions.

Details: [Sessions and devices](/docs/user/account/#sessions-and-devices).
