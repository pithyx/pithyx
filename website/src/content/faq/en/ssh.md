question: 'How do I enable SSH?'
category: security
tags: [ssh, shell, terminal, command line, root, console]

status: ready
---

SSH is off by default and root stays locked. An administrator gives the user the right "Sign in over SSH with a shell" and switches on **SSH logins** in Settings > **File services**. SFTP for file transfer is a separate switch and gives no shell.

Details: [SSH](/docs/admin/security/#ssh).
