/ˈpɪθɪks/, "PITH-iks". Pandora never opened a box: she opened a pithos (πίθος), a great storage jar of ancient Greece, which Erasmus of Rotterdam later mistranslated as pyxis, a box. What stayed inside was hope. Pithyx keeps what matters, on your hardware. The stem is pithos; the ending -yx is a stylization after Greek models such as onyx and Styx.
