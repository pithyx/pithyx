---
question: 'Can I install the system on a USB stick or SD card?'
category: hardware
tags: [usb stick, sd card, boot disk, system disk, 32 gb]
order: 40
status: ready
---

No. The installer offers only internal disks of at least 32 GB; USB disks, removable media and SD cards are not offered. A hard disk works, but the installer hints that an SSD is faster, quieter and uses less power.

Details: [Disks and pools](/docs/getting-started/requirements/#disks-and-pools).
