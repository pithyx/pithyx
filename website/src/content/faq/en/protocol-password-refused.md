---
question: 'Why is my password refused over SMB or SFTP?'
category: files
tags: [smb login, access denied, wrong password, sftp, webdav, ftp, network drive, credentials]
order: 60
status: ready
---

Usually one of three things: your account lacks the right for that service, the account is deactivated, or the service does not know your password yet. For the last one, sign in once on the web desktop, or ask an administrator to set a new password. After five wrong attempts within five minutes, further attempts pause for a short time.

Details: [Signing in](/docs/user/file-access/#signing-in).
