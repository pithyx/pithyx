Users cannot change their own password in this version: an administrator sets a new one in Settings > **Users** with **New password**, which signs you out everywhere. A password needs at least 8 characters and must not be on the list of widely used passwords; there are no rules about capitals or symbols.

Details: [Changing the password](/docs/user/account/#changing-the-password).
