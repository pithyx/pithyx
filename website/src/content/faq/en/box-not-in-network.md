---
question: 'Why does the box not show up under Network in Windows or in Finder?'
category: files
tags: [network neighborhood, discovery, mdns, bonjour, ws-discovery, not visible, explorer]
order: 50
status: ready
---

Pithyx does not announce itself on the network, so it does not appear in network browsing lists. Type its address instead: `\\<box-address>\` in Windows File Explorer, or `smb://<box-address>/` in **Connect to Server** on macOS.

Details: [Access files from other devices](/docs/user/file-access/).
