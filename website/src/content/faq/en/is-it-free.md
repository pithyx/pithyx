Yes. The system is free software under the AGPL-3.0, and the npm packages for app developers (`@pithyx/sdk`, `@pithyx/ui`, `@pithyx/cli`) are MIT. There is no paid edition and no feature behind an account.

Details: [License](/docs/project/license/).
