---
question: 'I set a wrong network address and lost the connection. What now?'
category: network
tags: [wrong ip, static ip, locked out, network reset, dhcp, console, rollback]
order: 10
status: ready
---

Wait 60 seconds: every network change goes back on its own unless you confirm it with **Keep**. If you are still locked out, use the console menu on the box's own screen: `[r]` resets every card to DHCP and `[s]` sets a static address, without a password.

Details: [The confirmation timer](/docs/admin/network/#the-confirmation-timer).
