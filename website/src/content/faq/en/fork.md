Yes, under the GNU AGPL-3.0, with the terms in `NOTICE`. A fork or redistribution keeps the attribution "Based on Pithyx (https://github.com/pithyx/pithyx), originally created by Schecher1 (https://github.com/Schecher1), licensed under the GNU AGPL-3.0." in `NOTICE`, wherever it appears in the source and docs, and on its About page. A modified version must use a product name of its own (not Pithyx) and not the Pithyx logo, and must not present itself as Pithyx. Apps built with the MIT-licensed npm packages are not affected.

Details: [License](/docs/project/license/#attribution-in-forks-and-redistributions).
