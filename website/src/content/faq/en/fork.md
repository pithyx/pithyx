Yes, under the GNU AGPL-3.0. The `NOTICE` file adds one condition under section 7(b): a fork or redistribution keeps the attribution "Based on Pithyx (https://github.com/pithyx/pithyx), originally created by Schecher1 (https://github.com/Schecher1), licensed under the GNU AGPL-3.0." in its source, its About page and its docs. You may use your own product name and logo, but say that it is based on Pithyx by Schecher1 and mark it as modified. Apps built with the MIT-licensed npm packages are not affected.

Details: [License](/docs/project/license/#attribution-in-forks-and-redistributions).
