Files draws thumbnails only for JPEG, PNG, WebP and GIF images up to 256 MiB and 50 million pixels; other types show an icon. The Viewer opens more formats, including PDF, text, and audio and video your browser can play.

Details: [Thumbnails and the viewer](/docs/user/files/#thumbnails-and-the-viewer).
