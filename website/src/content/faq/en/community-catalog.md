Yes, it is on by default, next to the official catalog. Its apps come from developers outside the Pithyx project; the project reviews, builds and signs them, but cannot vouch for them as for its own apps. To see only official apps, open the Store's gear and switch the community catalog off under **Catalogs**; switching it on again asks once more.

Details: [Catalogs and trust](/docs/admin/apps-and-store/#catalogs-and-trust).
