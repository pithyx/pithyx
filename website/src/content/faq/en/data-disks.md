question: 'Do I need more than one disk?'
category: hardware
tags: [disks, system disk, data disk, mirror, ssd, hdd]

Yes, for your data. The system goes on one internal disk, or on two as a mirror, and Pithyx never puts a pool on the system disk. Your files live on pools that you create on further disks in the Storage app.

Details: [Disks and pools](/docs/getting-started/requirements/#disks-and-pools).
