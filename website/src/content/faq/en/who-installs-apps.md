---
question: 'Can normal users install apps?'
category: apps
tags: [install app, store, administrator, user rights, app access]
order: 30
status: ready
---

No. Only administrators open the Store and install apps. For each app they choose **Who may use it**: everybody, administrators only, or selected groups and users. Ask an administrator if you miss an app.

Details: [App access](/docs/admin/permissions/#app-access).
