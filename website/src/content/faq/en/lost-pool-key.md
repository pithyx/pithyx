---
question: 'I lost the key of an encrypted pool. What now?'
category: storage
tags: [encryption, pool key, lost key, key vault, show key]
order: 31
status: ready
---

If the box still runs and keeps the key in its key vault, choose **Show key…** on the pool card, confirm with your password (and your authenticator code), and store the key again, or give the pool a new key with **Change key…**. If the key is not in the vault and every copy is lost, the data cannot be read again, by anyone: copy what you need off the pool while it is still unlocked.

Details: [Encrypted pools: when something goes wrong](/docs/admin/encryption-emergencies/).
