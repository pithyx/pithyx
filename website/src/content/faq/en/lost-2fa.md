---
question: 'I lost my phone with the authenticator app. How do I sign in?'
category: users
tags: [two-factor, 2fa, totp, authenticator, lost phone, recovery codes]
order: 40
status: ready
---

Choose **Use a recovery code instead** at sign-in and enter one of the 10 recovery codes you stored when you turned two-factor sign-in on; each works once. An administrator without codes can reset with the recovery key, which turns two-factor sign-in off. For other users the interface has no way yet to turn it off for them.

Details: [Two-factor sign-in](/docs/user/account/#two-factor-sign-in).
