In **Personal settings**: open the Pithyx menu, click your name and choose **Personal settings**, or open it like any app from the Pithyx menu. It has your account and security (two-factor sign-in, passkeys, trusting the device), the desktop (language, appearance, accent color, wallpaper, taskbar position, getting the desktop of another device), your default and sign-in apps and your notification channels. The account menu itself keeps only **Personal settings** and **Appearance**.

Details: [Personal settings](/docs/user/account/#personal-settings).
