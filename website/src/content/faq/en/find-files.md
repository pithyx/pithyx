---
question: 'How do I find a file when I do not know its folder?'
category: files
tags: [search, find, file search, search files, names, index]
order: 16
status: ready
---

Type part of its name in the Pithyx menu, or in the search field of Files and press **Enter**. The box searches the names in every shared folder you can open and in your own home, best matches first, and **Show all in Files** lists every match with its location. Only names are searched, not what is inside the files, and other users' homes are left out, also for administrators.

Details: [Searching every place](/docs/user/files/#searching-every-place).
