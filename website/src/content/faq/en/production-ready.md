Yes, with a backup. Pithyx 1.0 is marked beta: we consider it stable, but it has not yet proven itself on real boxes in everyday use. Treat it as beta and keep a copy of everything important on another device. Pools are not encrypted yet.

Details: [What is Pithyx](/docs/getting-started/what-is-pithyx/).
