---
question: 'Which app id should I use?'
category: development
tags: [app id, reverse domain, manifest, naming, org.pithyx]
order: 30
status: ready
---

Your domain reversed plus the app name, three to five parts, for example `com.mueller.chat` or `io.github.jan.chat`. Parts use lowercase letters, digits and hyphens. `org.pithyx.*` is reserved for system apps and the official catalog, and the box refuses it from a package file.

Details: [App ids](/docs/developers/apps/overview/#app-ids).
