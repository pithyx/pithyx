---
question: 'How do I reach my files from my computer?'
category: files
tags: [smb, sftp, webdav, ftp, network drive, windows, mac, linux, map drive]
order: 10
status: ready
---

Over SMB, SFTP, WebDAV or FTP, signing in with your Pithyx user name and password. SMB is on in a fresh installation and new users may use it; the other services an administrator switches on and grants. On Windows type `\\<box-address>\` in File Explorer, on macOS use `smb://<box-address>/`.

Details: [Access files from other devices](/docs/user/file-access/).
