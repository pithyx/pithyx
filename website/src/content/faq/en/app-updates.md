That depends on the policy on the Store's page **Updates**: **Install it**, **Tell administrators** (the default) or **Do nothing**, checked daily at 03:00 by default. An update that asks for new permissions never installs on its own. Before every update Pithyx takes a snapshot, and **Back to** with the version number returns to the previous version with its data as it was.

Details: [Updates and rollback](/docs/admin/apps-and-store/#updates-and-rollback).
