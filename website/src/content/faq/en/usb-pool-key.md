---
question: 'Can a USB stick unlock my encrypted pool?'
category: storage
tags: [encryption, pool key, usb stick, usb key, unlock, key vault]
order: 33
status: ready
---

Yes. Choose **Write key to USB stick…** on the pool card; any stick with a FAT, exFAT or ext file system works, and Pithyx never formats it. Whenever the stick is plugged in, the box unlocks the pool. You can pull the stick afterwards: the pool stays unlocked until the box restarts, and then waits for the stick again. Whoever has the stick and the disks has the data, so keep the stick apart from the box, and keep your copy of the key too. If the stick is lost, change the pool's key.

Details: [USB stick](/docs/admin/storage/#usb-stick).
