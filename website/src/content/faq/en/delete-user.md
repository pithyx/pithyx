---
question: 'Does deleting a user delete their files?'
category: users
tags: [delete user, remove account, home folder, deactivate, files]
order: 60
status: ready
---

No. The account goes, but the user's files and home folder stay, and the user id is never given to anyone else. To lock someone out without deleting anything, **Deactivate** the account: it loses every right and is signed out.

Details: [Deactivating and deleting](/docs/admin/users-and-groups/#deactivating-and-deleting).
