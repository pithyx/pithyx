---
question: 'I forgot my password. What now?'
category: users
tags: [password, forgot password, recovery key, reset, locked out]
order: 10
status: ready
---

Ask an administrator to set a new one; there is no reset by email. An administrator who forgot their own password chooses **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and enters the recovery key from the installation. Without the key and without another administrator, only a reinstall helps.

Details: [Forgotten password](/docs/install/recovery/#forgotten-password).
