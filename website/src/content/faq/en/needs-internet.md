---
question: 'Does Pithyx need an internet connection?'
category: general
tags: [offline, internet, local, lan, air gap]
order: 30
status: ready
---

No. The installer, the desktop, storage and file sharing work without internet. Only the Store fetches its catalogs and the container images of catalog apps from the internet, and the clock uses public time servers unless you set your own.

Details: [What leaves the box](/docs/admin/security/#what-leaves-the-box).
